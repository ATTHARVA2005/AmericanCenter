1. Market Understanding and Relevance

Evaluates how well the presentation explains the specific emerging market context, including local economic conditions, infrastructure, regulations, customer needs, and digital adoption trends.

2. Business Model and Commercial Viability

Assesses whether the AI business idea has a clear revenue model, realistic pricing, scalability potential, competitive positioning, and financial sustainability.

3. Application of AI Technology

Measures how effectively the presentation explains the AI solution itself, including its innovation, practicality, technical feasibility, data requirements, and suitability for the target market.

4. Social, Ethical, and Regulatory Considerations

Reviews whether the presentation addresses issues such as data privacy, bias, cybersecurity, accessibility, workforce impact, and compliance with local laws and ethical standards.

5. Presentation Quality and Communication

Evaluates clarity, organization, use of evidence or case studies, quality of visuals, audience engagement, and the presenter’s ability to answer questions effectively.
